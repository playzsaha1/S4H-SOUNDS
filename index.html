Privacy Standards

Your audio stays with you.

S4H-SOUNDS converts audio and generates metadata in your browser. We do not upload your audio files, filenames, or conversion contents to our servers.

Local processing and downloads

Your selected file, decoded audio, XML metadata and generated WAV stay in browser memory. Recent Downloads lists outputs from the current tab only. Refreshing or closing the tab clears this list. Files you download remain wherever your browser saves them.

Anonymous aggregate totals

After an output is successfully generated, the app may send a one-time random completion token to our counter service. The database stores only aggregate conversion and download-request counts. Conversions include WAV and XML exports. Both totals start at zero and are shared across users. The first download-button request for each confirmed export may increment the download total; repeat clicks do not. This measures requests, not files successfully saved. Requests after the 30-minute token expiry cannot be counted. On the local development server, restarting also expires tokens. Selecting a file, visiting, refreshing, failing or cancelling a conversion does not increment it.

Short-lived random tokens and overall request limits are held temporarily by the counter service to reject duplicate requests and reduce abuse. On the hosted service, these anonymous keys expire automatically in shared storage; only the aggregate totals are retained long term. Tokens are not linked to an account or device and expire within 30 minutes. Counter requests contain no audio, filenames, file sizes or metadata.

No personal analytics

The application does not store IP addresses or user identities, set tracking cookies, or load third-party fonts, scripts or analytics. Your theme preference may be saved locally in your browser. Network connections necessarily expose connection information to the hosting infrastructure; hosting and proxy logs must be disabled or configured separately by the operator.

When the counter is unavailable

Local exports still work. The interface reports when a completion cannot be confirmed by the counter; it never substitutes a made-up total. Unreported exports are not replayed after refreshing.
